# Course-wide References

Each lecture ends with focused sources. This page collects the references used most often across the course and favors textbooks, primary literature, and official software documentation.

The references may be retained in teaching copies and adaptations. Cite the course itself using [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff), and preserve source-specific citations when reusing or adapting material. Licensing and attribution requirements are stated in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
